How to Play

Drag bricks onto the wall

Pull the Next brick from the rack. It snaps into the nearest seat that works. You can also tap a seat.

Keep joints apart

A joint is the gap between two bricks. It must never sit right on a joint in the course below. Half bricks at the ends make this work.

Leave the openings

Dark gaps are windows and doors. Build both sides first, then cross the top with a brick long enough to rest on each side.

Park one brick in Hold

If a brick shows up too early, drag it to Hold and use it later. Hold keeps one brick.

Earn three stars

Finish with no undos and no hints. One or two slips earns two stars. Any finished wall earns one.
